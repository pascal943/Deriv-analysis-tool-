How to interpret this analysis

A local machine-learning model learns from earlier one-minute candles and checks later candles it did not train on. Rise/Fall setups need trend agreement, a strong model score, at least 30 qualifying test signals, a test result above chance, and an improvement over the baseline. Otherwise it abstains.

Scores are not calibrated probabilities. Recent test results do not establish future accuracy or profitability. The outlook starts at the last candle close; it is not the exact entry or expiry of a new contract. Payouts and execution costs are not included. Signals expire at the next minute boundary.
